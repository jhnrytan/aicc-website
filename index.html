LOCAL-FIRST CREATOR WORKFLOW

Create, review and organize your content with confidence.

AICC (AI Creator Command Center) is a desktop creator-workflow application designed to help users inspect videos, organize affiliate product information, prepare content drafts and review posts before publishing.

ABOUT AICC

A creator workspace that keeps people in control.

AICC brings content preparation tasks into one local workspace. It can help users review video files, compare content with saved product references, generate draft captions and hashtags, and maintain an approval and publishing checklist.

AICC does not guarantee audience reach, sales, affiliate commissions, product availability or platform approval. AI-generated suggestions should be checked by the user before use.

Video review

Inspect video files and review technical details before publishing.

Content preparation

Prepare draft captions, hooks, keywords and hashtags for supported platforms.

Affiliate organization

Keep product references and affiliate URLs organized for content workflows.

Human approval

Review content and confirm publishing decisions before sharing externally.
