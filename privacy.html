1. Who operates AICC

AICC (AI Creator Command Center) is operated by [INSERT YOUR FULL NAME OR LEGAL BUSINESS NAME]. Privacy questions and requests can be sent to [INSERT A MONITORED CONTACT EMAIL].

2. Information AICC may handle

Depending on the features you use, AICC may handle:

  • Video and media files: video files you select, filenames, file paths, technical metadata, sampled frames and optional speech transcripts.
  • Content drafts: captions, hashtags, keywords, product descriptions, affiliate URLs, approval notes and publishing schedules.
  • Product references: product names, categories, descriptions, reference images and public product-page metadata that you import or enter.
  • Workflow records: approval states, publishing history and performance figures that you enter.
  • Integration credentials: if you connect an authorized third-party integration, access and refresh tokens and related account identifiers may be stored locally in encrypted files.

3. How information is used

Information is used to provide the features you request, such as inspecting videos, transcribing speech, comparing video frames with product references, generating draft content, managing approvals, preparing publishing exports and recording performance information.

4. Local processing and storage

AICC is designed to run primarily on your computer. The application may store data in a local SQLite database and local folders. When configured for local Ollama models or local transcription, the related AI processing is performed on your computer. You are responsible for device security, operating-system access controls and backups.

5. Information sent to third parties

Some actions require network access. For example:

  • When you choose to connect or send content to TikTok through an authorized integration, the information needed for authorization, upload, draft transfer or publishing is sent to TikTok. TikTok processes it under its own privacy policy and platform terms.
  • When you import a public product URL, AICC may request publicly available page information from the relevant website. The website may receive ordinary connection information such as your IP address and request metadata.
  • If you configure or use an external AI, cloud, transcription or other third-party service, the information you submit to that service may be processed by that provider under its own terms and privacy policy. Review that provider's terms before enabling it.

AICC does not sell personal information. Do not add sensitive personal information to content drafts or product notes unless you have a lawful reason and an appropriate basis to do so.

6. TikTok authorization and credentials

If you connect TikTok using an official authorization flow, AICC may receive authorization tokens and an account identifier permitted by the scopes you approve. In the Phase 4 implementation, tokens are encrypted and stored in the local AICC installation's data folder. The encryption key is stored separately alongside the encrypted token file on the same device. Protect your Windows account and device; this local encryption is not a substitute for full-device security. You can disconnect the integration in AICC by deleting the locally stored connection, and you can revoke app access through TikTok's account settings.

Never share your Client Secret, access token, refresh token or local token key. AICC should not ask you to type your TikTok password into the application.

7. Retention and deletion

Local videos, database entries, drafts, product references, logs and integration records may remain on your device until you delete them. You control local retention and should remove records and files you no longer need. Revoking a third-party authorization prevents future authorized access but does not necessarily delete information already sent to that platform; contact that platform for its deletion process.

8. Security

We recommend using a password-protected device, keeping Windows and dependencies updated, limiting access to the AICC folder, protecting backups and never committing secrets or token files to a public source-code repository. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Children

AICC is not designed for children and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to this Policy

This Policy may be updated when AICC features or data practices change. The updated date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision. Review this page periodically.
